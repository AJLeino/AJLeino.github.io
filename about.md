# About

<p>I'm a data scientist and researcher with a strong background in quantitative analysis, statistical modeling, and data visualization. My work bridges the gap between rigorous analytical methods and real-world decision-making—whether that’s in public policy, economic research, or private sector insights. I specialize in turning complex datasets into clear, actionable narratives that help organizations better understand their users, operations, and strategic opportunities.</p>

<p>Over the years, I've worked with diverse data—from survey and panel data to administrative tax files and behavioral customer feedback—using tools like Python, R, SQL, Tableau, and Plotly. I’m particularly skilled in building end-to-end data pipelines, designing robust experiments, and developing dashboards that inform both technical and non-technical stakeholders. I value clarity, curiosity, and collaboration in my work, and I'm always excited to tackle challenging problems that require both analytical depth and practical thinking.</p>
